Date April 2026
Objective: Announce Intrest Media as a media education and consulting brand, establish authority using a decade of TMZ experience, and build the Tyson AI waitlist — using the existing audience of 800K+ across TikTok, YouTube, and Instagram as the launch runway.

5 Launch Videos

01
Post First — Authority

I Was Bureau Chief at TMZ. Here's What I Learned About Viral Content.

Opening Hook

"I spent over a decade at TMZ — I started as a producer and became Bureau Chief. Here's what nobody on the outside ever understood about why content goes viral."

What to Cover

Your career arc, told fast. Producer → Reporter → Senior Producer → Bureau Chief at one of the most powerful media companies in the world. What you learned about how stories get found, why some content explodes and most disappears, and the one thing TMZ understood that most creators still don't. End the video by introducing Intrest Media: "That knowledge is what I built this on."

Why it works: This is your credibility video. Post it first. It earns the right to teach everything that comes after. No one else in the content space has this background — lead with it.

02
Core Concept — Brand Message

You Don't Need More Followers. You Need the Right Content.

Opening Hook

"Stop trying to get more followers. The algorithm doesn't care how many you have anymore. Here's what it actually rewards."

What to Cover

Explain the core Intrest Media idea: we've moved from a follower economy to an interest economy. Show a real example — a creator with 2K followers outperforming someone with 200K, and explain exactly why. Break down what "interest-based distribution" means in plain terms. Don't sell anything. Just teach the concept. End by naming it: "This is what I call the Intrest Media era."

Why it works: This is the most shareable video in the series. The concept is counterintuitive and provable. If one video spreads the brand, it's this one.

03
Diagnosis — High Share Potential

You're Posting Every Day and Growing Zero. I Can Tell You Exactly Why.

Opening Hook

"If you're posting consistently and your numbers aren't moving, it's not your effort. It's one of these three things."

What to Cover

Give creators three specific, diagnosable mistakes: (1) Wrong content format for the platform — the algorithm treats a talking-head differently than a process video, (2) No clear interest signal in the first two seconds — the algorithm can't categorize what it can't read, (3) Posting for your current audience instead of your target audience. Each one fast, specific, and backed by what you saw inside TMZ's content operation.

Why it works: Diagnostic content gets saved and shared more than any other format. Creators tag other creators. This is how you reach new audiences beyond your 800K existing followers.

04
Business Owners — New Audience

You Own a Business and You're Not Posting. Here's What That's Costing You.

Opening Hook

"If you own a business and you're not creating content in 2026, you're invisible. But you don't need to go viral. You just need to be found by the right 500 people."

What to Cover

Speak directly to business owners who feel overwhelmed or left behind by social media. Reframe the entire goal: this isn't about going viral, it's about being discoverable by the specific people who will buy from you. Use a concrete, relatable example (a local service business, a coach, a restaurant). Show them that the algorithm will do the distribution — they just need to know what to post. Introduce Intrest Media as the answer.

Why it works: Business owners are an underserved audience in the creator space. This video opens a second lane of followers beyond the creators already watching you.

05
Product Launch — Tyson Reveal

I've Been Building Something. Meet Tyson — Your Personal Media Business Assistant.

Opening Hook

"I've spent the last year building an AI trained on everything I know about the media industry. It's called Tyson. And it's going to change how you think about content."

What to Cover

Introduce Tyson by name with a real screen demo — show a genuine question being asked and Tyson giving a specific, intelligent answer. Explain what makes it different: it's not a generic AI, it's trained on 10+ years of real media industry expertise from inside TMZ. Cover the two use cases (business owners and creators). End with a clear, single CTA: "Link in bio — get on the early access list. First 500 people in."

Why it works: Product reveal videos with live demos outperform explanation videos every time. The demo IS the proof. Don't describe Tyson — show Tyson working in real time.
