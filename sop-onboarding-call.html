Intrest Media — SOP #001

Client Onboarding Call Framework

Version1.0
Call Length60–90 Minutes
First UsedJune 15, 2026 — Caraline's Adventure
OwnerColin — Intrest Media
Purpose of this SOP: Use this framework for every new client onboarding call. It ensures every client gets the same high-quality experience, every important topic gets covered, and you leave every call with a clear action plan for both sides.
Before the Call
Pre-Call Checklist
Call Structure
Agenda — 60 to 90 Minutes
Minutes 0–5

Open & Set the Tone

Welcome them, celebrate that they took the step, and set the agenda for the call so they know what to expect.

Talk Track"Really glad you're here. Today we're going to build the foundation of your entire media company. By the end of this call you're going to have a content blueprint, a clear plan for your newsletter, and we're going to identify your first brand deal targets. Let's get into it."
Minutes 5–20

Brand Audit — Where Are You Right Now?

Pull up their profiles together. Walk through each platform. Ask them to share their analytics screen. Document the baseline numbers — this is what you'll measure growth against.

Questions to Ask"Walk me through your platforms — which one feels most natural to you? Where do you feel like you're closest to breaking through? What content have you posted that got the most traction, even a little?"
Minutes 20–30 ⚠️ Don't Skip

Email List & Newsletter Strategy

This is one of the most important conversations you'll have with every client. Social platforms can change their algorithm or disappear. An email list is the only audience they truly own. Frame it that way.

Talk Track"Here's something most creators never think about until it's too late — your followers on TikTok, Instagram, YouTube? You don't own those. The platform does. The moment you build an email list, you own that audience forever. No algorithm can take it away. So we're starting your newsletter from day one — not as an afterthought, but as a core part of your media company."

Cover: platform choice (Beehiiv recommended), cadence, content format, and the lead magnet or CTA you'll use to drive signups.

Minutes 30–40 ⚠️ Don't Skip

Bio Link Strategy

Every platform bio is valuable real estate. Most people waste it. Walk them through the exact strategy for what their bio should say and where the link should point.

Talk Track"Your bio is the only place on most platforms where you can put a clickable link. Most people either don't use it or point it somewhere random. We're going to be strategic — every bio, every platform, pointing to the same place with the same CTA. Here's how we think about it..."

The framework: One primary destination (newsletter signup or lead page). One clear CTA in the bio copy. Consistent across all platforms. Update it when you have a live offer.

Minutes 40–55

Content Blueprint — What Are You Posting?

Build their content pillars together. Identify 3–4 content categories that fit their niche, their personality, and what the algorithm is rewarding right now. Map out their first 2 weeks of posts.

Questions to Ask"What do you know better than most people? What could you talk about for an hour without notes? What do people come to you for advice about?"
Minutes 55–65

Brand Deal Targets

Identify 5–10 brands they'd love to work with. Then identify 5–10 realistic brands that are a natural fit for their current audience size. Build the hit list together.

Talk Track"Brand deals don't just happen — we build a pipeline and we work it. Let's start with your dream list, then we'll get realistic about what we can close in the next 90 days based on where your audience is right now."
Minutes 65–75

Audience Tracking Setup ⚠️ Don't Skip

Set up the system for tracking where growth is coming from after each post. This is how you double down on what works and stop wasting time on what doesn't.

Talk Track"Every time you post, we're going to track three things — which platform the post went on, what type of content it was, and where your new followers and clicks came from that day. Over time this tells us exactly what your audience responds to and who they are. That's how we build a machine, not just a posting schedule."

Cover: what metrics to check, when to check them (24hrs and 7 days post), and how to log them in the tracking sheet.

Minutes 75–90

90-Day Roadmap & Next Steps

Close the call with a clear picture of what the next 90 days look like. Both sides should leave knowing exactly what they're responsible for.

Talk Track"Here's what the next 90 days look like. Month 1 is about foundation — content system, newsletter live, bio links locked in. Month 2 is about momentum — brand deal outreach, audience growth, doubling down on what's working. Month 3 is about monetization — closing deals, building your speaking pipeline, turning your audience into income."

After the Call
Action Items — Both Sides
Colin — Intrest Media

Send call recap email within 24hrs

Summarize what was decided, the 90-day roadmap, and both sides' action items.

Client

Set up newsletter platform account

Sign up for Beehiiv (or agreed platform) and share login access.

Colin — Intrest Media

Build content calendar — first 2 weeks

Deliver the first 14 days of posts with topics, formats, and hooks.

Client

Update all bio links

Implement the bio link strategy across every platform before first post goes live.

Colin — Intrest Media

Create audience tracking sheet

Build the client's tracking doc and share it. Walk them through how to use it.

Client

Send brand wish list

Email Colin their top 10 brands they'd love to work with before end of week.

Colin — Intrest Media

Start media kit

Begin building the client's media kit using their current stats and niche positioning.

Client

Post first piece of content

Using the content blueprint — first post goes live within 48 hours of the call.
